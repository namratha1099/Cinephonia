# 🎬 Cinephonia – Guess the Film by Its Music

## Hear the song. Name the film.

Cinephonia is a movie music guessing game. A song plays for 15 seconds, and you pick which film it's from.

## How to play
Choose who's playing: just you, you against a bot, or friends on the same device.
Pick a film industry: **Tollywood** , **Bollywood**  or **Hollywood** .
Choose a difficulty and how many scenes to play.
Listen, then pick the right film as fast as you can.

Answer within the first 5 seconds for a ⚡ lightning bonus and a little celebration.

## Party mode

Play with friends on their own phones or laptops.

1.One person taps Host a party and gets a room code.
2.Everyone else taps Join with a code and types it in.
3.The host starts the game, and everyone hears the same song at the same time.
